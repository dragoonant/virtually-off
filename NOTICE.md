# Notice

**Virtually Off is an original, unaffiliated, non-commercial work by evilgouki.**

It is a spiritual successor to a genre: one-on-one 3D arena mech fighters with twin-stick controls, dashes and lock-on
shooting. It is not associated with, endorsed by, or licensed from any publisher of such a game.

What this project does not contain: no names, characters, machine designs, stage layouts, music, sound, voice or
interface art from any published game. The working title is a pun the owner chose knowingly. Game mechanics such as
dashing, jumping, lock-on and weapon gauges are methods of play, not creative expression.

What it does contain: machines from the owner's own Assembly Forge project, which were generated for that project from
original briefs (Hunyuan3D, under Tencent's non-commercial community licence). Skies, textures and props were
generated locally with FLUX models. Music and effects were generated with ACE-Step and Stable Audio Open (Stability AI
community licence), and the announcer with Qwen3-TTS voice design. It is non-commercial throughout.
